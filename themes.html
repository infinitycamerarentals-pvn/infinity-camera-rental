Before you book

The questions crews ask first.

What ID is required for pickup?

A government photo ID: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, or driving licence. Company rentals can use a GST certificate or letterhead.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes. A refundable deposit is taken at pickup, usually 50–100% of the gear value, and returned when the kit comes back in the same condition.

Do you deliver in Hyderabad?

Yes. Delivery is arranged on WhatsApp. Same-day pickup is available at the Manikonda office.

How do I pay?

UPI, bank transfer, or cash, collected at pickup along with the deposit.
